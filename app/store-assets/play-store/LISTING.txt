Ahona is a health and beauty app for Bangladesh: genuine medicines, lab tests with home sample collection, and video or audio doctor consultations — in one place.

MEDICINES

• Express delivery in 12–24 hours

LAB TESTS AT HOME
• CBC, HbA1c, lipid profile, dengue NS1, thyroid, and more

• Book from the app — our team collects the sample at your address

DOCTOR CONSULT

• Instant or scheduled slots
• E-prescription after the consult
